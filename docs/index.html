Calendar Search for Excel

An internal Excel task pane add-in for searching and summarising the 2027 Schedule sheet.

What it does

  • Campaign Search – find text on the calendar and jump to the cell.
  • Destination Summary – slots, sold, open and rate-card totals per placement.
  • Open Placements – every unsold slot for a destination.

Privacy & security

  • Static files only: no server, database, sign-in, cookies or tracking.
  • Workbook data is read inside Excel and shown only in your own task pane. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • The add-in never edits cell contents. It only moves your selection when you click a result.
  • The only external script is Microsoft's Office.js from appsforoffice.microsoft.com.
